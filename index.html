Sustainable fitness

I specialise in helping people build a sustainable fitness lifestyle — not just chasing short-term results.

Every programme is tailored to your goals, experience, and lifestyle, making fitness realistic, enjoyable, and achievable. Whether your goal is to get stronger, improve your health, lose body fat, or simply feel more confident, I'll help you build habits that last. I'm passionate about showing that fitness is for everyone — and that investing in your health can improve every aspect of your life.

Based at CrossFit Jona in Portadown, I coach one-to-one in person and online.
